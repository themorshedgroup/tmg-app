Troubleshooting
⏳
Can't see the app after signing in?
Your account is being set up — you'll have access within 24 hours and receive an email notification once it's ready.
🌐
"Add to Home Screen" isn't there
You're probably in an in-app browser (the preview inside Instagram, Facebook, or Gmail). Those can't install apps — open the link in Safari or Chrome directly.
📵
App opens in browser, not full-screen
Tap the TMG App icon on your home screen — don't open it from a browser bookmark or link. On iOS 26, make sure Open as Web App was on when you added it.
🔍
Can't find the icon after installing
Swipe through all your home screen pages, or search "TMG App" using iPhone Spotlight (swipe down from the home screen).
💬
Still stuck?
Message Symon in the TMG App team chat, or email manager@themorshedgroup.com with a screenshot and your iOS version.
