How to use the Calls tab

A guide for TMG sales agents. Updated 24 September 2026.

The Calls tab is your call list, pulled live from Zoho. It shows who you are due to call, hands you their number, and lets you record the call without ever opening Zoho.

The Calls tab on a desktop browser. One row per scheduled call: who they are on the left, their number and email in the middle, the three action buttons on the right.

Two things to know before anything else:

  • Everything here comes from Zoho and goes straight back into Zoho. It is not a separate list you have to keep in sync.
  • Every call and every KPI you record is credited to you by name. KPI just means the activity numbers you are measured on.

The buttons across the top

Capacity and Add KPIs on the left, the view switcher and refresh on the right, the day strip underneath.

  • Capacity (the navy button) opens a month view showing how many calls land on each day. Use it to see a pile-up coming before it arrives. It has its own back arrow.
  • Add KPIs (the outlined button) records activity for anybody, whether or not they are on today's list. This is the one to use at the end of the day.
  • 3-Day / Week / Overdue switches which list you are looking at.
  • Guide opens this page over the list, so you never have to go looking for it. There is an "Open in a tab" link in its header if you would rather keep it beside your calls.
  • The circular arrow refreshes the list from Zoho.

Choosing the day

In 3-Day view you get Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow, each with the number of calls on it. Clicking one switches the list. In Week view you get all seven days with their dates, and clicking a day scrolls it to the top rather than filtering, so the week reads as one continuous list. Saturday and Sunday are dimmed.

Overdue

Pick Overdue in the same drop-down to see every call still open from before today, in one stack. The number beside it in the drop-down is how many there are.

Overdue: oldest first, a divider per due date saying how many days late it is and how many are still open on it.

  • There is no day strip here. These are not somebody else's problem tomorrow, so there is nothing to navigate between. It is one list, oldest first.
  • The three buttons work exactly as they do anywhere else.
  • Ticking one does not make it disappear. It stays where it is with a line through it, so you can undo the tick if you pressed it by mistake. The number in the drop-down counts the ones still open.
  • Anything you log from an overdue row is dated today, not the old due date, because today is when you actually did it.

You do not have to work from this list. Anything still open from before today is also the first thing the app will close for you when you log a call from anywhere else.

Reading a row

One row, left to right.

AI Button: Opens a summary of that person before you dial: their deals, the last thing that actually happened, and every email on their Zoho record. Nothing is saved, it is built fresh each time.
Name: A link that opens their Zoho record in a new tab.
Client Classification: A, B or C. This is what sets how often they are due a call.
No class: Nobody has set a classification yet. Three dots mean it is still loading.
EO: An EO touch call. It is a kind of call, not a classification.
Prospect Form Type: Click it to see and edit the form.
Contact Tags: Typed into Zoho by a person. Every tag is shown, never hidden behind a "+3", because the one that gets hidden is always the one that says Do Not Call.
Spouse: A gold heart and a name means that person has a spouse on file. Spouses are listed one after the other, because you work a household as one conversation.
Phone Number: An sp. chip means the number belongs to their partner, not to them. "No number" means there is nothing on file for either.

The three buttons on every row

Tick: Marks the call completed in Zoho. Press it again to put it back to Not Started. If Zoho refuses the change, the row keeps its old state and tells you why.
Plus: Logs other activity against that person: notes, hotzone actions, pop-bys, lunches. Greyed out if the row has no contact attached.
Phone: Dials. If the number belongs to the spouse, the tooltip says so before you press it. A crossed-out phone means no number is on file.

Logging what happened, from the row

Click the plus and a box opens in the middle of the screen. Tick what you did, set a count where one is needed, and press Add.

Once something is logged, a small green strip appears under the row listing what went in. Logging again adds up rather than replacing.

After logging a note and two hotzone actions: a green strip under the row showing exactly what was filed.

Recording your KPIs

Press Add KPIs at the top. You get two routes.

Add KPIs opens over the list: fill out a form, or paste your notes.

Route 1: Fill out a form

One person, their KPIs ticked. "Everything entered here is credited to Kyle Baird" is the line that proves it is yours.

  1. Check the KPI Date. It defaults to today.
  2. Enter CTC Hours if you have any, otherwise leave it blank.
  3. Type the person's name in Person 1. It suggests contacts as you type.
  4. Tick the KPIs that apply: Touch Call, Follow Up Call, Notes, Hotzone Action/s, Pop-by, Lunch.
  5. If you tick Hotzone Action/s, enter how many.
  6. Press Add another KPI for the next person.
  7. Press Add other KPIs for anything not in that list. You get a drop-down of Zoho's own options and a count. There are only three of these slots.
  8. Press Review and Confirm.

The form will stop you if a person has a name but no ticked KPI, or a KPI but no name, or an Other KPI with no count. Those used to be dropped silently. Now it says so.

Route 2: Paste your notes

Type or paste what you did in plain English and let the app pull the KPIs out of it. You land on the same review screen either way.

The review screen

This is where every KPI is matched to a real Zoho contact. You get one card per person with their name, the contact the app matched them to, and their KPI chips.

  • If the match is right, do nothing.
  • If it is wrong or missing, press change and pick the right person.
  • Nothing can be sent until every person is linked to a contact. That is deliberate: a KPI with no contact cannot be proved to be yours.

Press Confirm and send when the card reads correctly.

Too soon for a touch call

A touch call is the cadence call, the scheduled catch-up the whole classification system is built on. If you claim one only a couple of weeks after the last conversation, it inflates the single number the cadence is measured by, and Zoho raises a chore for somebody else to go and validate it a week later.

So the app checks for you. On the review screen, if you tick Touch Call for somebody who was called too recently, the chip changes to Follow Up Call and you get a gold note telling you why, before you send.

Touch Call became Follow Up Call, with the reason spelled out and the next touch date given.

The call is not lost and you are not being accused of anything. It still counts. It just counts as what it actually was, and what you see on screen is exactly what lands in Zoho.

The cadence intervals are:

A call made a few days early still counts as the touch, because cadence dates get pushed off weekends and holidays. A first call to somebody with no history is always a real touch call.

Capacity

Press Capacity to see the months ahead and how many calls land on each day. Agents see their own months only. Use it to spot a heavy day while you can still do something about it.

Two months at a time on desktop. Shaded days are heavy, a cross is a weekend or a holiday.
